# APEX-7 Player Playtest Verdict

Seat: @player. Method: played the **built** game (`?race=<stage>`) in headless
Chromium (WebGL2/SwiftShader) and sampled per-car telemetry every 100 ms through
`AiRaceScene.snapshot()`, plus a filmstrip of frames. Raw data:
`docs/evidence/APEX-7-playtest.json`.

## What I did

- Watched a full four-car AI race on `alpine-forest` and `canyon-sprint`.
- Measured, from the live rendering loop: how many cars are on screen, whether any
  left the road, the closest two cars ever got, and speed on corners vs straights.
- Captured frames at ~1 s, 8 s, 16 s and 24 s (`APEX-7-playtest-<stage>-t<sec>.png`).

## Result

| Stage | Cars on screen (mean) | Off-road | Closest gap | Corner vs straight speed | All finish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| alpine-forest | 4.0 / 4 | 0 | 2.26 m | 15.6 vs 20.0 m/s | yes |
| canyon-sprint | 3.85 / 4 | 0 | 2.26 m | 13.9 vs 16.0 m/s | yes |

- **Corner braking is natural.** Cars clearly slow before the tight sections and
  build speed on the straights on both stages; the difference is visible in the
  frames and in the numbers.
- **You can watch the race.** The spectator camera frames the pack, so multiple
  cars (usually all four) stay in shot instead of only the leader.
- **They don't drive through each other.** The closest approach over a whole race
  is ~2.26 m, comfortably more than a car width (1.8 m).
- **They stay on the road.** Zero frames with a car beyond the ribbon edge.

## Defect found and fixed during playtest

The first playtest run failed: on `alpine-forest` two cars closed to **1.49 m**
at the finish. Cause: the *visible scene* stopped finished cars in place instead
of parking them, so finishers queued on the line and the next car drove into them
(the headless simulator already avoided this). Fixed by sharing the simulator's
parking helper (`parkOffLineCar`, now with distinct lateral slots) with the scene,
and by framing only the racing cars in the camera. Re-run is clean (gap 2.26 m).

## Verdict

**PASS — the race is watchable and credible.** All acceptance criteria hold from
the player's seat: multiple cars complete the stage on-surface, slow for corners,
accelerate on straights, avoid contact, finish close, and the experience reads as
a race.
