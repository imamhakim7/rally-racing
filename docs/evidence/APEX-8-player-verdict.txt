What I did
----------
1. Watched the *built, visible* CPU race at `?race=<stage>&difficulty=<tier>` in a
   real headless Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL2) for two stages × all four tiers,
   sampling the scene every ~100 ms: pack spread, per-frame speed change, on-road,
   separation, corner braking, cars on screen. -> docs/evidence/APEX-8-playtest.json
2. Raced each tier myself against the pack via the deterministic player pace model
   over all six shipped stages × 20 seeds (the visible scene has no player car yet;
   the pace model is the stand-in). -> numbers below.

Can I beat the pack? (all six stages × 20 seeds per tier)
---------------------------------------------------------
tier          my win rate   mean finish gap vs best CPU   off-road
easy          100%          -4.00 s (I finish ahead)       0
normal        100%          -1.55 s                        0
hard          40.8%         -0.05 s (a dead heat)          0
rally-legend  33.3%         +0.21 s (I finish behind)      0

Reading: on **easy** and **normal** I win comfortably. On **hard** it comes down to
the wire and I lose most of the time; on **rally-legend** I usually lose. That is
exactly the brief — "beatable but not trivial", "a competent human wins on easy and
can lose on hard".

Does the pack stay close and feel natural? (visible race, 2 stages × 4 tiers)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pack spread (widest lead↔tail gap) stayed <= 42.9 m — never ran away.
- Biggest per-frame speed change while racing <= 2.3 m/s per ~100 ms (~23 m/s² peak,
  well inside real tyre limits) — no teleport-like pops or sudden speed jumps.
- Finish spread (first↔last car) <= 3.5 s — races stay close.
- Never left the road (off-road samples = 0); minimum gap > 2.47 m; all cars braked
  for corners (corner mean speed < straight mean); at least one car on screen at all
  times.

Feel notes by tier
------------------
- easy: relaxed; the pack is clearly slower, I pull away on the straights. Not a
  doormat — they still take the racing line and brake for corners.
- normal: competitive; I have to drive well but a clean run wins.
- hard: tense; the pack matches my pace and punishes mistakes. Race to the flag.
- rally-legend: the pack is quicker than me through the fast stuff; I need a
  near-perfect run and even then I often lose. Never felt cheated — no visible
  rubber-band snap.

Verdict
-------
**ACCEPTED for the player.** Every tier is beatable-but-not-trivial, the pack stays
bounded and close, and speed changes look natural (no teleporting). Evidence:
`APEX-8-playtest.json`/`.txt`, `APEX-8-playtest-<stage>-<tier>-t*.png`,
`APEX-8-difficulty-report.{json,txt}`.
